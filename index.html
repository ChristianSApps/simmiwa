Your Profile

Customize your profile to suit your needs:

  • Enter your height in feet or centimeters
  • Your weight can be in kilograms, pounds, or stone
  • Record your body fat data as a percentage or in the same unit as your weight
  • You can choose the same for muscle mass
  • Please pay attention to your blood sugar level, whether in mg/dl or mmol/l
