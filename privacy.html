Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. General Information

Protecting your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy informs you about how the iOS app Simmiwa handles data.

The app was designed so that no personal data or other user data is transmitted to us or to third parties. Data generated by the app or entered by the user remains exclusively on the device used.

2. No Collection of Personal Data

We do not collect, store, or process any personal data of our users on our own servers or third-party servers.

In particular, the app does not transmit data such as:

  • Name or email address
  • Contact details
  • Location data
  • Device or advertising IDs
  • Usage or tracking data
  • Photos or media files
  • Content that you create or save within the app

to us or other companies.

To the extent that the app processes such data, this is done exclusively locally on your device and only for the purpose of providing the respective app functions.

3. Data Storage

All data stored within the app is stored exclusively on your device. We have no access to this data and do not receive any copies of it. You can delete the locally stored data within the app, in the General section under Delete all content and settings, or by deleting the app from your device.

4. No Sharing with Third Parties

User data is not shared, sold, or rented to third parties. The app does not use any third-party services that transfer user data from the app for analysis, advertising, tracking, or profiling purposes.

5. No Tracking or Advertising

The app does not use tracking to monitor users across different apps or websites. No personal data is transmitted to third parties for personalized advertising or advertising measurement.

6. Data Security

Since the data processed by the app remains exclusively on the device, we do not store any user data on our own servers. The security of locally stored data depends, among other things, on the security measures of the Apple device used and its operating system. We recommend protecting the device with a secure passcode and keeping the latest software and security updates up to date.

7. User Rights

Since we do not collect or store any personal data from users on our servers, we generally do not have any personal user data that we could provide, modify, or delete upon request. Users retain control over data stored locally in the app. This data can be removed from the device, either within the app or by deleting the app, provided the respective app function allows it.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or legal requirements change. The current version of this Privacy Policy is available at the privacy URL provided for the app.
